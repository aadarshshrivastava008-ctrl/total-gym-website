Facilities

Two floors. One standard.

Level 1 is the performance hall: Eleiko competition platforms, a 40-meter sprint track, and a 25-meter resistance pool. Level 2 is recovery: infrared sauna, contrast plunge, NormaTec, and a quiet work loft overlooking the floor.

  • 12 Eleiko Olympic platforms and calibrated kilo plates
  • Hammer Strength plate-loaded circuit and Prime selectorized line
  • Assault, Echo, SkiErg, and Woodway 4Front cardio bay
  • Turf field for sleds, med-ball work, and change-of-direction
  • Private assessment room: InBody 970, force plates, VALD dynamometry
  • Members’ locker rooms with steam, cold rinse, and recovery nutrition fridge

Programming

Classes with a purpose, not a playlist.

Forge Strength

45-minute barbell sessions. Linear periodization, weekly 1RM tracking, and a 1:8 coach ratio. Monday squat emphasis, Wednesday press, Friday pull.

Length & Load

Mobility plus loaded stretching for hips, T-spine, and ankles. Designed as the companion to heavy lower-body days, not a standalone stretch class.

Aether Conditioning

Zone 2 blocks and short glycolytic finishers. Heart-rate straps required. Saturday long piece is 50 minutes on the track and SkiErg.
