Terms & Conditions

Why is Access to USB Storage Devices Restricted?

USB storage devices, especially USB Flash Drives are notorious among hackers due to inexpensive cost and portability. Plugging a USB Storage Device in Raymond Ltd (referred as organization hereafter) - owned assets can introduce security breaches, malware, or unauthorized data transfer. As per Raymond IT Security Policy, all USB ports are blocked across company endpoints. Access requires business head approval and is provisioned remotely by the department Linux Administrator based on asset hostname for 3 to 4 hours only, after which the port is automatically re-blocked.
